the shift

Aligned with Sustainable Development Goal 8 (SDG 8): Decent Work and Economic Growth, this project examines youth employment uncertainty amid AI-driven changes in creative industries. Concerns about job displacement provide a context for Tang Ping ("lying flat"), a response to intense professional pressures (Ren et al. 2026). Drawing on Wu Wei, the Daoist principle of non-forcing action, the project explores adaptation without resistance or passive withdrawal.

  • SDG 8: DECENT WORK & ECONOMIC GROWTH

“Promote sustained, inclusive and sustainable economic growth, full and productive employment and decent work for all.” (United Nations 2015)

Gilbert F. Houngbo's statement, “The future of work will not be determined by technology alone” (ILO 2026), underscores the role of human decisions and institutions in shaping decent work. Through two p5.js generative artworks and a supporting interactive webpage, the project visualises the relationship between human agency and technological change. Its call to action, “Work with change, not against it,” encourages young creatives to develop their skills, use emerging technologies purposefully and participate in shaping their professional futures.
